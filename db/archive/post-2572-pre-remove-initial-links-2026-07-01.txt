<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Is the Difference between Leading vs. Managing?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>While leading and managing both involve strategy, problem-solving, decision-making, and collaborating with others, there are distinct differences:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Set the vision and direction</li>
<li>Focus on long-term organizational growth</li>
<li>Inspire change and innovation</li>
<li>Motivate and develop people</li>
<li>Take risks and push boundaries</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Oversee day-to-day tasks and operations</li>
<li>Ensure policies and plans are executed</li>
<li>Organize, coordinate, and administer</li>
<li>Enforce structure and maintain order</li>
<li>Focus on consistency and efficiency</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In short, leading centers on the big picture – energizing people and driving growth through new ideas. Managing centers on administrative tasks – enforcing standards and coordinating workflow to ensure smooth operations. Understanding these complementary roles helps organizations cultivate strong leadership and management.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">17 Leadership Training Topics for Effective Corporate Leaders</h2>

<h3>1. Leadership Styles</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Understanding different leadership styles is essential for aspiring leaders of any organization. According to HubSpot, there are<a href="https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/leadership-styles#types-of-leadership-1" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> 11 main leadership styles</a>, including but not limited to transactional, democratic, strategic, and bureaucratic, with each style having its own strengths and weaknesses.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Including the varied leadership styles in your training not only helps the company's current and future leaders become familiar with leadership fundamentals but also helps budding leaders determine how to lead their teams with the most efficiency.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>2. Effective Communication</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Communication is the key to success and a productive work environment. <a href="https://uk.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/effects-of-lack-of-communication" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Lack of communication in the workplace</a> leads to conflict and distrust. <!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Effective communication training may include the cultivation of different skills such as active listening, empathy, clarity, and transparency. This puts to use effective communication channels and many other techniques. By offering training on these topics, you have a better chance of improving and fostering effective communication in the workplace.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>3. Team Building and Management</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A good leader knows the value of team building and understands it's not just a means to an end. Instead, it's an<a href="https://hr.berkeley.edu/hr-network/central-guide-managing-hr/managing-hr/interaction/team-building/introduction#:~:text=Team%20building%20is%20an%20ongoing%20process%20that%20helps%20a%20work%20group%20evolve%20into%20a%20cohesive%20unit.%20The%20team%20members%20not%20only%20share%20expectations%20for%20accomplishing%20group%20tasks%2C%20but%20trust%20and%20support%20one%20another%20and%20respect%20one%20another%27s%20individual%20differences." target="_blank" rel="noopener"> ongoing process</a> that involves building a cohesive unit that trusts and values its team members, respects individual differences, and utilizes their strengths to accomplish tasks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A<a href="https://teamstage.io/team-building-statistics/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> recent study</a> highlights that about one-third of employees believe effective team building and emphasizing collaboration increase employee retention and foster loyalty.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That's why when training future leaders of your organization, it's important to include team building and management as a part of the training program. Exploring different team formation stages and understanding team strategies and collaboration techniques should be integral to the training.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>4. Diversity and Inclusion</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Research shows that organizations with a diverse team bring in around <a href="https://builtin.com/diversity-inclusion/diversity-in-the-workplace-statistics#:~:text=Diverse%20Companies%20Have%202.5%20Times%20Higher%20Cash%20Flow%20Per%20Employee" target="_blank" rel="noopener">2.5 times higher cash flow</a> per employee. It's no secret that more organizations are adopting a diverse and inclusive work environment. So, educating leaders of the company on diversity and inclusiveness is extremely important.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Raising awareness about topics such as unconscious racial or gender bias or using inclusive language through training can help combat stereotypes and create a more inclusive and productive work environment.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>5. Employee Wellbeing and Experience</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The performance of employees is directly proportional to their well-being and experience. Research shows that <a href="https://teamstage.io/motivation-statistics/#:~:text=Employees%20work%2020%25%20better%20when%20motivated." target="_blank" rel="noopener">employees work around 20% better</a> and more efficiently when motivated.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The art of inspiring and motivating team members is a skill that all leaders need to learn as part of their training program. By working on the experience of the employees and putting some thought behind their well-being, a good leader can boost productivity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>6. Coaching and Mentoring Strategies</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Coaching is an excellent strategy to boost employee morale and facilitate conversations among team members. A good leader should know how coaching and mentoring can benefit their team.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Why? Because<a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbescommunicationscouncil/2022/05/19/does-mentoring-still-matter-for-fortune-500-companies/?sh=6bfc62d45d8c" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> 84% of Fortune 500 companies</a> are doing it. If you don't, you're likely to fall behind.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>By opening a one-to-one communication channel, a leader can develop a better working relationship with the employee. It also allows you to easily track progress, offer appraisals, and monitor performance. Understanding the ins and outs of<a href="https://www.ninjatropic.com/blog-how-to-plan-create-effective-employee-training-videos-step-by-step-guide/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> effective employee training videos</a> may also help with the process.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>7. Strategic Thinking and Problem-Solving</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Problems in the workplace are inevitable. And when the company boat's in troubled waters, it's up to the leader to step up. Having the patience and prudence to examine a problem from different angles is arguably one of the most important skills for a leader.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With strategic thinking, leaders can improve execution and implementation, which seems to go south in nearly all companies. A<a href="https://hbr.org/2017/11/executives-fail-to-execute-strategy-because-theyre-too-internally-focused" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> Harvard Business Review</a> report highlights that only a dismal 33% of well-formulated strategies become successful.</p>

<h3>8. Project Planning and Delegating</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Planning projects and assigning responsibilities is an essential skill that all leaders must have. The job of a corporate leader is to oversee the project to ensure its success.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A leader needs to master project management basics such as creating project plans, setting milestones, distributing resources, managing team morale, delegating tasks among team members, and everything else needed to accomplish the project.</p>

<h3>9. Goal Setting</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To lead effectively, a leader must set a clear goal for their team. Research shows that employees with a clear objective are <a href="https://collato.com/blog/organizational-goal-setting-statistics#:~:text=Employees%20with%20goals%20are%203.6%20times%20more%20likely%20to%20be%20committed%20to%20their%20organization" target="_blank" rel="noopener">3.6 times more likely</a> to stay committed to their organization. <p>Training corporate leaders in goal setting will help them understand how to set proper objectives for their employees. By implementing proper goals for themselves and team members, a leader can achieve cohesive results and boost employee performance.</p>

<p>Virtual leadership training topics include effective remote communication, the use of online tools to steer projects to completion, emotional intelligence toward different cultures, data-driven decision-making, and automation.</p>

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<p>That is why it's important to integrate digital training as part of leadership training. With a clear grasp of the basics of the organization's digital arsenal, a leader can provide better feedback and valuable insights.</p>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>12. Change Management</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Every organization goes through change at certain points. While it's a part of organizational growth, it can be difficult for employees. Fear of the unknown, adjusting to the new workflow, and other issues can present new challenges for employees.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If a leader is skilled in change management topics, they can guide team members through these changes. Through effective communication, understanding the psychology of change, and providing support and feedback, a skilled leader can make this transition easier.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>13. Conflict Resolution</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Conflict in the workplace can happen at any time for any reason. Dissatisfaction with the work, unfulfilled needs, or even disagreements with fellow employees are a few examples of how workplace conflict festers. What makes a leader truly stand out is how he handles the situation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When conflict in the workplace gets in the way of efficiency, it's the job of the leader to intervene. Strong problem-solving skills with a keen sensibility to relate and communicate with the employees will help resolve these conflicts in a calm, productive way.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>14. Crisis Management</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If the COVID-19 pandemic taught us anything, it's that crisis can strike at any time. Learning how to manage and handle crises of all forms is an integral part of leadership training topics. Whether it's a natural disaster, supply chain disruption, or a large-scale cyber attack, a leader should be able to assess and control the situation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An effective leader must have <a href="https://www.everbridge.com/blog/7-skills-leaders-must-master-for-effective-response-to-critical-events/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">the essential skills</a>, such as situational awareness, decisiveness, and improvisation, to manage a crisis situation. Delivering proper training on these skills will help your future leaders handle corporate challenges with ease.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>15. Time Management</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Managing time is another important skill that all leaders should develop. Leaders often have to deal with multiple projects at the same time. One option for leaders to manage their project deadlines efficiently is to work on their time management skills.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An effective leader also extends his time management skills to employees by encouraging them to work smarter, not harder. This leads to a better work environment for the employees since they're not rushing to meet deadlines but rather have a steady pace when it comes to accomplishing tasks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>16. Ethics and Social Responsibility</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No leadership training would be complete without an understanding of the ethics and social responsibilities of the organization. Doing things the right way matters, and a leader needs to be able to make the right call.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ethical training will equip leaders with the knowledge to make judgment calls, whether it's related to product sustainability, treating employees fairly, or protecting the privacy of customers. When things get stressful and complex, a leader needs to ensure his choices are moral and align with the organizational goals.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>17. Personal Development</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Personal development should never take the backseat for a leader. Continuous self-assessment and growth are essential to being a great leader. They lead by example, and by looking inward, they can evolve their leadership techniques. However, even leaders burn out – compared to 60% in 2020, around<a href="https://www.ddiworld.com/global-leadership-forecast-2023/ceo-challenges" target="_blank" rel="noopener"> 72% more leaders feel drained</a> in 2023.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That's why personal development training is essential to withstand the mental and physical exhaustion of leadership.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Personal development topics are an integral part of leadership training. It involves improving skills like mindfulness, self-awareness, and emotional intelligence. With a focus on these elements, a leader can identify blind spots in his leadership and achieve a wider perspective.</p>
