<p>🔸 <strong>Instructional:</strong><br>Step-by-step videos are essential for patients to learn new skills and techniques. They can cover everything from self-care routines to medication management.</p>

<p></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>🔸 <strong><strong>Conceptual</strong></strong>:<br> Animated videos are great for breaking down complex medical information into easy-to-understand concepts. They can explain diseases, treatments, and preventative measures.</p>
