<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="title1">B2B Training Done Right: 7 Essential Components Every Quality Corporate Training Video Production Company Must Have</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To be able to choose the right training video production company, you need to first know what to look for, right? Below is your 7-point checklist to know you're in the right hands.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>1. Expertise</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It goes without saying that the company you choose should have expertise in corporate training videos. They're different in terms of objectives and content, so you want to be sure that the company you're looking at understands the nuances of training videos.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What's more, if possible, you should look for a company that has experience in your specific industry. For instance, if you need retail training videos, safety training videos, or something similar, the more they know the subject matter inside-out, the better.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>2. Cost-Effectiveness</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Corporate training videos can get expensive. To save costs, look for a video production company that has the tools and resources to complete your project without breaking the bank. It's also important to make sure you have clarity on what's included in their services – from pre-production planning to post-production editing. Aim for the highest value for your money.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>3. Client Experience</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>References and <a href="https://www.ninjatropic.com/customer-testimonials/">testimonials</a> are your best friends when it comes to choosing the right video production company. A good track record tells you that they provide a great client experience, so don't hesitate to ask for direct feedback from previous clients and take your time to read through reviews.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>4. Communication</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It's crucial to have an understanding of how the company works and what its process looks like. Do they clearly discuss timelines, deliverables, costs, etc.? Do they provide you with updates during the process? It’s also important to find out if they’ll be open and responsive to any feedback you might have during the project.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>5. Video Portfolio & Reviews</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It's easy to go on and on about how great certain training video production companies are, but the proof of their excellence lies in the quality of their work. Ask to see some of their previous work – if they have a <a href="https://www.ninjatropic.com/elearning-video-portfolio/">portfolio</a>, even better! And again, look for reviews from industry professionals and former clients to make sure you're not signing up for sub-par quality.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>6. Custom-Tailored Services</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No two corporate videos are the same, so you'll want to make sure that your video production company can provide custom-tailored services.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ask them if they'd be willing to put together a package specifically designed for your project, or if they have any suggestions for how to best address your needs. One-size-fits-all solutions rarely work, so you'll want to make sure your production company is willing to meet your specific requirements.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>7. Turnaround Time</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Lastly, make sure to ask about turnaround time. If you have a tight deadline, then it's all the more important that your chosen video production company can deliver in a timely manner. On top of that, find out if they provide any revisions and determine how long those might take so you don't run the risk of missing your deadline.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
